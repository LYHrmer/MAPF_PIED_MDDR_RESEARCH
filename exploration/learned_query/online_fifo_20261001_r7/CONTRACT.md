Reuse the unchanged official OnlineGGO OBJ3 persistent MAPFPlanner bridge and configuration, followed by the existing exact shared Geometry/Index/PositionCommit and full-cap ReferenceController. Public committed plan frontiers are planner state, not measured robot positions. Four-move maximum outstanding lookahead; no all-agent physical-END barrier. Cell visits preserve their official order: an incoming MOVE waits for its prior occupant departure to START, then its exact swept resources must pass ordinary owner readiness. A simultaneous cyclic rotation can be unsupported by this conservative adapter; retain the resulting failure, never replace the author planner.

Current FIFO head alone is public to planner and actor. A head is serviced only at its true original goal at rest and is replaced only after normal END. A frontier at the current goal retains that old goal until actual service/END, even if official planner waits or moves it aside. An ample fixed private reservoir (256 tasks/agent) prevents finite-third-head parking. Private eta is persistent sign with 9:1 noise, indexed by actual MOVE ordinal; actor receives only normal-END history, declared law and public commitments. Actual original END and delivery(+1/4) remain separate. Unit full-cap original controller is unchanged.

Different policies can release different task counts; this secondary is not a common-fixed-task makespan.
