Public inputs, private replay checkpoints, author replies, actual commands, stdout/stderr, timings and full compressed event/segment traces are retained.

Primary descriptive outcome is sum completion time; makespan is always reported separately. Success metrics distinguish returned graph, legal adoption with changed type2 orientation, changed executed control/geometry and changed completion outcomes. These reused instances are interface mechanisms, not independent heldout planner performance. No learned model, future oracle, parameter selection, path replanning or real-time controller claim.
